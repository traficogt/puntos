# Native app quality — 2026-11-10

PF keeps its Bricolage Grotesque, Fraunces and Inter fonts and brand palette. The app uses task-focused screens instead of the website’s marketing layout: loyalty card and QR first, four persistent labeled destinations, concise rewards and chronological activity, and grouped account settings. The compact rewards tab is labeled “Premios”; its screen title remains “Recompensas”.

## Applied guidance

- [Apple: designing for iOS](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/designing-for-ios) and [tab bars](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/tab-bars): stable top-level destinations, clear labels, focused primary actions, native back/close and safe areas.
- [Android core app quality](https://developer.android.com/docs/quality-guidelines/core-app-quality): new controls meet a 48dp minimum; text wraps and respects system scaling; selected states and accessible labels are explicit; contrast, offline/retry states and denied permissions are considered. Wide layouts use a navigation rail.
- [Apple App Review guidance](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/): privacy/support and account deletion remain inside the app; notifications require an explicit action and permission, with access unaffected by denial. Demo mode is labeled and creates no account or real QR.

## Sign-in and data behavior

Existing customers enter their phone directly. Only successful phone verification reveals business memberships. A single card opens automatically; multiple cards require selection with a single-use, five-minute proof held only in memory. A new card still requires the business’s registration link/code. Invalid or expired codes explain how to retry. Cooldowns survive Back; abandoned requests cannot restore a session or open demo mode later.

Customer sessions remain business-scoped. Native credentials use SecureStore; cached balances exclude credentials and customer contact information. Cached information is labeled and does not authorize QR generation or reward redemption. Missing snapshots show loading/retry states, rather than claiming there are no rewards or transactions.

## Verified for this change

- App lint, TypeScript, 60 unit tests.
- Browser fixtures: phone-first single/multiple/no-card login, grant expiry/rejection, canceled verification, cooldown navigation, business enrollment, missing-data recovery.
- Browser navigation and captures at 320, 390, 430 and 1024 pixels, light/dark themes; offline and stale rewards, QR expiry and session expiry. No real verification message or customer transaction is generated by fixtures.
- Backend review and isolated PostgreSQL coverage verify OTP attempt limits, one-time proof, atomic session creation, tenant isolation, deletion races and real messaging/logging integration with a local test provider.

## Device and distribution checks still required

Browser captures do not establish native keyboard, VoiceOver/TalkBack, Dynamic Type, gesture, real device performance or push-delivery behavior. Check those on an iPhone and Android device before public release. iOS signing/build acceptance is recorded separately in CODEMAGIC.md. No Android package or Google Play publication is claimed by this UI change. Store privacy disclosures and review approval remain separate release requirements.

Verification-code delivery requires the configured messaging phone to be connected. Server-side fixture delivery is not a successful real WhatsApp test.
