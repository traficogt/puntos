# Pertenece — visual direction

Scope: `https://pertenecegt.com/`, with source assets in `public/credenciales`. Mode: Persuade. Existing PF homepage and apps retain their own design.

The owner sells membership credentials to organizations with existing member lists. The page supports a salesperson: identify the service, demonstrate its fit and capture a qualified demo request. The owner confirmed the standalone brand Pertenece and domain pertenecegt.com. Pricing remains undecided. The independent site runs on port 3100 behind the edge proxy; the old PF preview remains excluded from indexing.

Retain Fraunces for display, Bricolage Grotesque for brand and headings, Inter for body and controls. A warm association prospectus: paper, forest-green credentials, clear rules, open composition, lime emphasis and peach contact section. The credential is original HTML, not a screenshot claimed to be a live Wallet. All illustration data is fictitious.

Tokens: paper #f5f4ec; ink #203d35; secondary #53635b; forest #183e35; lime #d9e9a7; line #cdd2c4; contact #e8d4bf. Maximum width 1440px, responsive 22–72px side spacing, sections 60–110px. Controls at least 44px high. Product radii 14–16px; action controls 6px. One interaction switches example verification among active, expired and offline with text as well as color.

Direction exploration: membership welcome pack, community noticeboard, association prospectus (concept-seed assignment 3), reception desk, exhibition catalog, annual member report and sports club program. Prospectus carries both introduction and precise operational caveats. Theatrical lighting/cloud color/silkscreen/woodblock/teletext challengers do not improve membership clarity; retain their discipline of distinct states, focused hierarchy and color commitment, not their motifs. The detailed user brief governs structure and code-led implementation.

Research: Society of Operations Engineer on Awwwards informs membership clarity; Dwell informs type scale and pacing; HoneyQuote informs CTA hierarchy and reassurance. These are nominee references, not endorsements. No source artwork copied.

Limits belong in copy: QR does not prove identity; a saved card does not guarantee current validity; offline checks cannot approve current status; spreadsheets do not synchronize automatically. Service is in preparation. Demo requests use PF's contact service; verification is a browser-only simulation. Stripe checkout follows an agreed scope and price.

Naming and sales copy: use Pertenece in branding, metadata and lead messages; retain PuntosFieles only as truthful parent-team/legal attribution. Contact: hola@pertenecegt.com. Use Precios for pricing navigation. Emphasize membership cards, existing member lists, guided setup and the reception status example. The owner's updated direction removes Guatemala, Spanish-language support and quetzal pricing as headline selling points. Do not present membership validation as identity verification or the simulation as an operational service. Official Wallet badge artwork illustrates the invitation step and has no live save action on this landing page.
