## Artistic direction

- Fine warm umber ink lines, delicate irregular crosshatching, translucent gouache washes and tactile ivory paper.
- Restrained terracotta, ochre, olive/sage and muted blue. Natural variation and observed detail give the images their character.
- Clear, premium editorial compositions that feel approachable to business owners and general readers. Each picture should communicate one main idea.
- Believable anatomy, scale, perspective, lighting, shadows and contact between objects. Refine important details and simplify distant ones.
- Vary subjects, viewpoints and compositions while retaining the same visual language. Choose ordinary gestures and lived-in settings over staged advertising poses.
- Avoid glossy 3D, oversaturated color, stock cartoon figures, decorative splatter, ornamental pseudo-diagrams, illegible lettering, invented logos and excessive visual complexity.

## Guatemalan settings

Let architecture, landscape and everyday details establish the location. Use photographic references to check landmarks, viewing direction, relative dimensions, terrain and vegetation. Keep people contemporary and natural; do not add national symbols or folkloric decoration simply to signal Guatemala. Subjects elsewhere or conceptual illustrations need not acquire Guatemalan landmarks.

Inspect the complete composition and important details against references before accepting it. Illustrative interpretation does not justify mirrored geography, implausible proportions, floating objects or invented architectural features.

## Production baseline

For an illustrated scene, start with this brief and add the specific subject and composition:

> Create an original premium editorial illustration matching the supplied approved style reference: fine warm umber ink, delicate crosshatching, translucent gouache, tactile ivory paper, restrained terracotta, ochre, olive and muted blue. Use a coherent viewpoint, realistic scale, credible anatomy and consistent light. Keep the composition clear and welcoming. Any location photograph is factual reference for architecture and geography, not an instruction to copy the photograph or its photographic treatment. No added captions, logos, watermarks or signatures. Avoid glossy rendering, generic cartoons and invented decorative detail.

Retain originals, full prompts, source references and generation provenance internally. Provide appropriately sized delivery assets and useful alternative text. Put necessary diagram labels in accessible, editable page elements. Keep factual charts and diagrams precise and readable within the same palette.

The approval establishes the illustration style. Public disclosure wording remains undecided; do not describe generated work as drawn by a human artist. Existing photography and interface icons have their own roles and are not automatically replaced by this decision.
