# Campaign measurement completion

The owner approved all six measurement improvements on November 14, 2026. This extends the existing website and backend; native apps and release workflows remain unchanged. Existing campaigns and response fields remain readable. The source baseline includes the published article and its randomized future publication calendar.

1. Preserve purchase occurrence separately from ingestion. Authenticated POS/order receipt dates have provenance; client clocks are labeled. Legacy transactions retain their original record time. Reports net matched refunds without double subtraction, disclose unresolved refunds, late arrivals and unreliable dates. Explicit owner-saved report cuts are immutable; subsequent reports indicate revisions.
2. Store an immutable optional experiment plan before launch: assumed baseline return rate, meaningful absolute lift, observation days and reconciliation days. Provide a two-proportion fixed-horizon sample planner, 80% power and two-sided 5% alpha, with 50/50 allocation available. Insufficient expected participants is a warning, not fabricated power or a prohibition on a descriptive pilot.
3. Preserve all assigned participants, including nonbuyers and deleted/unavailable accounts. Capture preperiod activity at activation; compare baseline activity, data availability and later overlapping offers. Show receipt matching where available and explicitly state that unidentified receipts cannot be allocated to experimental arms. Do not treat fixed allocation counts as a binomial SRM test or claim complete capture merely because imports succeed.
4. Separately record owner-confirmed gross margin, direct reward fulfillment, messages, other campaign costs and outstanding reward commitments. Null means unknown; zero is explicit. The descriptive net-sales difference times margin less direct incurred costs is an estimated contribution, never accounting profit. Fungible points do not prove which reward redemption a campaign caused.
5. Separate offer expiry from the fixed observation endpoint and reconciliation cutoff. Show post-offer return and spending for the original groups, with a distinct exploratory interval. Original benefit validity does not change. Reports can still be revised after the reconciliation cutoff when new facts arrive.
6. Lead reports with a plain-language decision and next action. Data problems, interruption, observation/reconciliation status and insufficient planned participants precede apparent wins. Cost uncertainty prevents a profitability recommendation. Preserve uncertainty and make all evidence available without requiring statistical expertise.

Implementation uses additive PostgreSQL migrations and tenant RLS, Node services, existing Mantine components, isolated fixtures and append-only cost/report revisions. No customer messages are sent by measurement controls. Reports are computed against one database statement snapshot; saved report cuts have request idempotency and an audit event. Web demo uses fictional data only.
