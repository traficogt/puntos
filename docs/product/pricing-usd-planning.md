# PF pricing comparison and cost planning

Owner clarification, 2026-11-11: compare subscription pricing, competitors, and operating costs in USD. Publish intentional local prices; do not treat the merchant's loyalty transaction currency as the PF subscription billing currency.

## Proposed, not approved for sale

The owner's supplied proposal anchors the entry tier at GTQ199/month, with GTQ149–299 as a discussion range and GTQ1,990 annually. GTQ99 introductory pricing, a permanent free tier, and a 300–500 member cap are alternatives, not decided offers. Existing provisional GTQ149/399/999 rates are withheld from the public plans page. Existing billing settings and entitlements are unchanged by this website release.

The proposal's approximately USD26 equivalent for GTQ199 is an illustrative conversion supplied by the owner, not a checked current FX quotation. A pricing decision needs a dated GTQ-per-USD exchange-rate input, including conversion/settlement fees. Compare all tiers using the same rate and period. Do not automatically derive local published prices from fluctuating spot rates.

## Decision worksheet

For each candidate tier, record:

- Published local monthly/annual amount, billing currency, tax treatment, annual discount, effective dates and target country.
- USD comparison amount = local amount / local currency units per USD, with rate source and date.
- Fixed cost allocation per active merchant: hosting, database, backups, monitoring, Wallet infrastructure, licenses, and support time at an explicit hourly cost.
- Variable costs: messages by provider/channel, payment fees, storage/traffic and support beyond the allowance. Use observed costs when available; label assumptions.
- Expected subscription contribution = net subscription receipts less allocated operating and payment costs. Model low, expected and high use; do not count free build minutes as a permanent subsidy.
- Free/intro offer eligibility, duration and maximum exposure, plus the upgrade and discontinuation policy, before publishing such an offer.

Merchant value is separate from PF's unit economics. Additional sales are not profit or evidence of causation. Illustrative monthly merchant contribution:

    additional visits × average ticket × contribution margin
    − additional reward fulfillment cost
    − PF subscription fee
    − other incremental costs

Under those stated assumptions, break-even additional visits:

    (subscription fee + fixed incremental costs)
    / (average ticket × contribution margin − additional reward cost per visit)

A nonpositive denominator has no finite break-even result. Avoid double-counting costs already included in the margin. If visits are only observed rather than experimentally attributable, describe the result as a scenario, not revenue generated by PF.
